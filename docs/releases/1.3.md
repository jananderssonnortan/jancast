## Jancast 1.3

- **MP4 starts as fast as MKV.** MP4 and MOV files now play in about a second instead of being prepared first.
- **It says why when it can't.** When a file has to be prepared first, the player says why, for example "AV1 video is re-encoded" or "the file is still downloading".
- **A new icon.** A TV showing a film's colour timeline, easy to spot in the Dock.
- **A look of its own.** With no film loaded, Jancast shows its dark room and warm screen glow. Play and Pause glow in the film's colour, each Recent item's progress has its own film's colour, and the disk image matches.
- **Space plays what's selected.** Space plays the file highlighted in Recent, or pauses it if it's the one playing.
- **A now playing bar.** While something plays and you look at another file, a bar at the bottom shows it, with Pause and the way back to the player.
- **Room for Diagnostics.** The remote is a little wider, and Diagnostics opens as a full-height panel beside it when the screen has room (under it otherwise).
- **Steadier and softer edges.** Pressing Play or Stop no longer moves the window's contents around, and lists fade out at the bottom of the window instead of being cut off.
