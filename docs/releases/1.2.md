## Jancast 1.2

- **Series remember your choices.** Audio language, subtitles and subtitle delay carry over to every episode of the same show, not just the next one.
- **Sleep timer.** Stop playing after 15 minutes to 2 hours, from under the tracks while playing or *Playback → Sleep Timer*. Resume picks up where it stopped.
- **Keyboard everywhere.** Tab moves between the TV menu, the buttons, Audio, Subtitles and the library, also while playing; Return or Space uses what has focus. ⌥↑ / ⌥↓, Home / End and Page Up / Page Down move through the library.
- **Updates.** Jancast checks for new versions once a day (you can turn it off in Settings) and installs them with one click, showing what's new first.
- **Calmer layout.** Switching between files no longer moves the remote around, and the Play button stays put.
- **A friendlier start.** The disk image shows what to do, and the first start after installing or updating may take up to a minute without giving up.
