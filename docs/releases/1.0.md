## Luftbro 1.0

Luftbro plays your own video files, MKV included, on an Apple TV, with the Mac as the remote.

- **Plays what the Apple TV can't open.** MKV and other files are turned into a stream it can play while you watch. Video and audio are copied as they are, so an MKV, MP4 or MOV starts in about a second. Downloads that are still in progress play too.
- **Audio and subtitles, any time.** Switch tracks while playing. Text subtitles and Blu-ray picture subtitles (read as text by macOS) both work, and a subtitle delay is one pick, or H / J in half seconds. Each file keeps its delay; each series remembers your audio, subtitles and delay.
- **Subtitles that fit.** Subtitles → Delay → Sync Automatically listens for the speech and moves the subtitles to match it, and stretches subtitles that drift (made for another frame rate) so they fit from start to finish.
- **Skip Intro and Next Episode.** A button during an episode's intro and when its credits start, found by their sound and in the picture when the file has no chapters. Space presses it while it's new, ⏭ any time, and the timeline marks where they are.
- **A proper poster.** The file's cover art when it has one, else the series' or film's title card, found by reading the opening titles.
- **The film as colours.** The timeline is the film drawn as bands of colour, with a frame preview when you hover it; the window takes its background and accent colour from the film.
- **Diagnostics.** A panel that slides out beside the window: the likely cause of a stutter (network, encoder, Apple TV, source file), live charts, every segment request and an event log. It records all the time, so you can open it after something went wrong.
- **This Mac.** Play in a window on the Mac instead, with Picture & Sound (brightness, contrast, saturation, Fit / Fill, volume) and subtitles that stay clear of the controls.
- **A remote that stays out of the way.** One narrow column with the library underneath, browsing it while something plays; TV volume, media keys, Control Center, a sleep timer, a Touch Bar on the film's colour, and every key in Help → Keyboard Shortcuts (⌘/).
- **Private by default.** The backend answers only to Luftbro, each stream has a secret address, and your watch history and Apple TV pairing are readable only by your account.
